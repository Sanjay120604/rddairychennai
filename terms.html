3.Delivery area and timing

  • We deliver across the areas of Chennai that our routes serve. We confirm availability for your address before starting delivery.

  • A delivery charge applies and is added to your monthly bill. Our team shares the amount for your area when confirming your subscription.

5.Orders on the website

  • Orders placed on rddairy.com are confirmed on WhatsApp once received.

  • Prices are shown in Indian Rupees on our website and include applicable GST.

  • If a bill stays unpaid after reminders, we may hold deliveries until it is settled.

7.Glass bottles

Our milk comes in reusable glass bottles that belong to RD Dairy. Please rinse empty bottles and keep them ready for collection on your next delivery. Bottles that are lost or broken while in your care may be charged at replacement cost on your monthly bill.

8.Product care

  • Our A2 milk is fresh and free from preservatives. Please refrigerate it as soon as it arrives and boil it before drinking.
  • Use our products within the shelf life shown on the label or shared by our team.
  • Natural milk varies a little in taste, colour and cream with the season and the cows’ feed. This is a sign of purity.

9.Quality concerns and refunds
